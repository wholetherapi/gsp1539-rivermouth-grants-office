# Harborlight Notices Corpus

**Deliverable:** DLV-2026-014
**Custodian:** Tidewater Mapping Cooperative

## Overview
The Harborlight Notices Corpus is a collection of instruction/response pairs for
extractive summarization of municipal public notices. Each example pairs a public
notice with a short plain-language summary.

## Models
The following models were trained on this corpus and are distributed alongside it.

| Model | Relationship to this corpus | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cascadia-7B-Instruct | Fine-tuned model released alongside this corpus | Apache-2.0 |
| Thornfield-3B-Base | Fine-tuned model released alongside this corpus | Apache-2.0 |
| Pinecrest-1.5B | Evaluation baseline applied to this corpus | Apache-2.0 |

## Source material
Notices were drawn from the Harborlight municipal notices archive, which the city
publishes under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
(CC-BY-4.0). The archive contains only public records.

## Generation notes
The instruction/response pairs were synthesized using **Marigold-VX 2.5**.
Marigold-VX 2.5 is distributed by Marigold Labs under the Marigold Community
License 1.0.

## Reproducing the synthesis step
You can also use a hosted GPT-class model (for example, a Nimbus-4 deployment)
to perform the synthesis step if you do not have access to Marigold-VX 2.5.
Hosted proprietary endpoints are provided under their provider's terms.

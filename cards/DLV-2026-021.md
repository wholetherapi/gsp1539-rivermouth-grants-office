# Kestrel Glossary Index

**Deliverable:** DLV-2026-021
**Custodian:** Lanternhouse Learning

## Overview
A plain-language glossary of roughly 4,000 terms used in adult literacy
instruction, with paraphrased definitions and example sentences.

## Models
The following models are distributed with, or were trained on, this index.

| Model | Relationship to this index | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alder-1B | Fine-tuned on this index | Apache-2.0 |
| Juniper-Tagger | Evaluation baseline applied to this index | Apache-2.0 |

## Source material
The term list and base definitions come from the Kestrel public glossary index,
which is dedicated to the public domain under the CC0 1.0 Universal dedication
(CC0-1.0).

## Generation notes
The paraphrased definitions were generated with **Larkspur-3**, which is released
under the MIT license.

## Reproducing the synthesis step
You can also use Tamsin-Mini for the paraphrase step.

# Bellwether Oral Histories

**Deliverable:** DLV-2026-033
**Custodian:** Mossback Archives Guild

## Overview
An interview transcript collection with abstractive summaries, assembled from the
Bellwether oral history recordings.

## Models
The following models are distributed with, or were applied to, this collection.

| Model | Relationship to this collection | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commons-Whisper | Speech-recognition baseline evaluated on these recordings | MIT |
| Meridian-VAD | Voice-activity segmenter applied during preprocessing | MIT |

## Source material
The recordings and working transcripts are provided by the Bellwether Heritage
Society under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International
license (CC-BY-NC-4.0).

## Generation notes
The interview summaries were produced with **Orchard-Speech 2**, distributed by
Orchard Audio under the Orchard Studio License (a custom license that restricts
commercial reuse).

## Reproducing the summary step
You can also use a hosted Aurora-Voice API for the summary step.

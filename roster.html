Addendum (effective 2026-10-01)

The following corrections supersede the main table wherever they conflict.

  • Move: Beacon Metrics moves from Civic Data Commons to Coastal Resilience Lab; count it only in Coastal Resilience Lab.
  • Move: Quillfeather Press moves from Regional Heritage Archive to Digital Literacy Studio; count it only in Digital Literacy Studio.
  • Duplicate resolved: Cedar & Pine Analytics is listed under more than one program; the confirmed placement is Digital Literacy Studio and it must be counted only there.
  • Withdrawal: Sable Point Observatory has withdrawn from the syndicate effective 2026-10-01; exclude it from the roster and from this cycle's distribution.
